## 1. Find tasks

![The Tasks list](1-list.webp "Search and filters at the top")

- [[1]] Search by words, and pick a status and a priority.
- [[2]] **My tasks** shows only yours. Or pick a project, a person or a label.
- [[3]] **Export CSV** downloads what you see, for a spreadsheet.

Green titles are done and red titles are overdue.

## 2. Change many tasks at once

![Many tasks selected](2-bulk.webp "Tick the boxes, then pick what to do")

1. Tick the boxes on the left [[1]].
2. **Set status**, **Assign to** or **Delete** [[2]] changes them all at once.
3. The **×** clears the selection.
