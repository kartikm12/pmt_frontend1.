## 1. Open the form

Click **New Task** on a project's board, or on the **Tasks** list.

## 2. Fill it in

![The New task form](1-form.webp "The details on the left, who and when on the right")

1. **Task title** and **Description** [[1]]: what needs to be done.
2. **Status** and **Priority** [[2]]: Low, Med, High or Urgent.
3. **Project** [[3]] first, then **Assign To**: only people on that project can be picked.
4. **Start date** (optional) and **Deadline** [[4]].
5. **Planning** [[5]] (optional): labels such as *bug* or *design*, and whether it repeats.
6. Click **Create Task**, or press **Ctrl Enter**.

The people you assign get a notification and an email.

:::tip Repeating work
Weekly reports and other routine work can repeat on their own: see [Repeating tasks](/help/tasks/repeating-tasks).
:::
