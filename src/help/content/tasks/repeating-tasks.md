![Repeat choices](1-repeat.webp "Pick how often it comes back")

1. In the task form, open **Planning** and click **Repeat** [[1]].
2. Pick how often [[2]]: every day, every weekday, every week, every 2 weeks or every month.
3. Save the task.

When you finish the task, PMT creates the next one with the next due date, for the same people. A repeating task shows a small **Weekly** (or similar) tag on its card.

To stop it, edit the task and set **Repeat** to *Doesn't repeat*.
