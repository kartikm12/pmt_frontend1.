## 1. The checklist

![The checklist](1-checklist.webp "Small steps inside one task")

- Tick a step when it's done [[1]]. The count at the top shows how many are done, and the card on the board shows it too.
- Type a step and click **Add** [[2]] to add one.

## 2. Comments

![Comments](2-comments.webp "Type @ to tell someone")

1. Write in the box [[1]]. Type **@** and pick a name to mention someone: they get a notification.
2. Attach files with **Attach** [[2]], drag them in, or paste a screenshot.
3. Click **Post**.

You can change or delete your own comments. **Activity** shows the task's history and **Files** lists everything attached.

:::note View-only people
People with *View only* access to the project can read comments but not write them.
:::
