Click a task's title anywhere to open its page.

![A task page](1-task.webp "Status, details and the people on it")

- [[1]] **Status**: click to move the task to another column, the same as dragging its card.
- [[2]] Priority and due date. *in 3 days* or *2 days late* tells you how close it is.
- [[3]] **Edit** changes the title, description, people, dates and labels.
- [[4]] **⋯** has **Copy link** (to share the task) and **Delete task**.
- [[5]] The side panel: who's assigned, the start date, labels and followers.

:::faq What is "Waiting on"?
If a task can't finish before another one, the side panel shows *Waiting on* with that task. When the other task is done, it's crossed out.
:::

:::faq Who are followers?
People who are told when this task changes stage or gets a comment, besides the people assigned to it. A project [rule](/help/projects/rules) can add followers, for example a lead for every urgent task.
:::
