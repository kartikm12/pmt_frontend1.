## 1. Open the form

Go to **Team** and click **Add Member**.

## 2. Fill it in

![Add Team Member](1-form.webp "Their details, role and a temporary password")

1. **Full name** and **Email address** [[1]].
2. **Role** [[2]]: see [Roles](/help/team/roles). **Job title** is just a description, such as *Frontend Developer*.
3. **Temporary password** [[3]]: at least 8 characters.
4. **Assign project** (optional) and **Reports to** [[4]]: their manager. See [Reports to](/help/team/reports-to).
5. Click **Add Member**.

:::warning Give them the password yourself
PMT doesn't email the new person. Tell them their email and temporary password, and ask them to change it in **Settings → Password** after they sign in.
:::

Colleagues can also sign up themselves; then you approve them. See [Approve sign-ups](/help/team/approve-sign-ups).
