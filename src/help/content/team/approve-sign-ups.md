When someone signs up themselves, you get a notification, and they wait at the top of the **Team** page.

![Waiting for approval](1-waiting.webp "Pick their role, then Approve or Reject")

1. Pick their role [[1]].
2. Click **Approve** [[2]]. They get an email saying they can sign in.
3. Or click **Reject** [[3]] if you don't know them. Their sign-up is removed.
