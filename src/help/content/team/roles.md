## What each role can do

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything, including project rules, the Audit log, and retiring or deleting assets |
| **Manager**, **Project Manager** | Create projects, manage every project, add and approve people, the asset register (for people who report to them), Recently deleted |
| **Developer**, **Designer**, **Tester**, **Support Engineer**, **Business Analyst**, **Team Member** | Work on the projects and tasks they're part of. All the same in PMT: the name just says what they do |

Inside one project, anyone can also be made that project's Manager: see [The project team and project roles](/help/projects/project-team).

## Change a role

![Edit Team Member](1-edit.webp "Role, job title, project and manager")

1. On **Team**, click **⋮** on the person's row, then **Edit Member**.
2. Pick the **Role** [[1]] and click **Update Member** [[2]].

The new role applies straight away.

## Suspend or delete

The **⋮** menu also has **Suspend Member** (they can't sign in until you make them active again) and **Delete Member**.

:::note Only Admins change Admins
Managers can't edit or remove an Admin.
:::
