**Reports to** is a person's manager. It decides which managers look after that person's company assets: a manager sees the assets of everyone who reports to them, and to their reports, and so on.

![Reports to](1-reports-to.webp "A yellow notice counts people without a manager")

- A yellow notice [[1]] tells you how many people don't report to anyone yet. Until you set it, only Admins see their assets.
- Each person's manager shows under their name [[2]].

To set it, click **⋮ → Edit Member** on the person's row, pick **Reports to**, and click **Update Member**. PMT won't let two people report to each other.
