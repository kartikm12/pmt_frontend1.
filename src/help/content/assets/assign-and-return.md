## 1. Assign an asset in stock

![Assign](1-assign.webp "Pick the person; their projects follow")

1. Open the asset and click **Assign**.
2. Pick the person [[1]]. The project list shows only their projects; with one project, it's picked for you.
3. Pick the **Condition when assigned** [[2]] and click **Assign**.

The person is told and confirms with **I received it**. Until then the asset shows **Not confirmed**.

## 2. Return it

![Return](2-return.webp "Back to stock, or straight to repair")

1. Open the asset and click **Return**.
2. Set the date and the condition it came back in [[1]].
3. Choose **Return to Stock** or **Send for Repair** [[2]], and click **Return**.
