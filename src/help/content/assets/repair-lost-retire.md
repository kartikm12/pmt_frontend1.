The buttons on an asset's page change with its status.

![The asset's buttons](1-buttons.webp "What you can do with this asset now")

## Send to repair

1. Click **Send to repair** [[1]].
2. Describe the problem and, if you like, the repair centre. Click **Send to repair**.
3. When it's back, click **Repair done**: give its condition, then return it to stock or assign it again.

## Mark lost

Click **Mark lost** [[2]] if it can't be found. Its status becomes *Lost* and it counts under **Lost** in the register.

## Retire or delete (Admins)

- **Retire**: it's sold, scrapped or too old. It stays in the history but can't be assigned again.
- **Delete** [[3]]: removes it from the register. It can be brought back from **Recently deleted** on the Assets page for 30 days.
