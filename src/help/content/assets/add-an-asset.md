On **Assets**, click **Add asset**.

## 1. What it is

![Add asset, the details](1-details.webp "The tag is made for you")

1. Pick the **Category** [[1]]. The **Asset tag** (such as *ENT-LAP-0004*) is made for you.
2. **Asset Name** [[2]], then brand, model and serial number. A serial number can only be used once.
3. **Condition** [[3]], where it is, its specifications, and photos.

## 2. Warranty and who gets it

![Add asset, warranty and assigning](2-warranty.webp "Pick the warranty period in months")

1. **Purchase date** and **Warranty period** [[1]] (No warranty, or 3 months to 5 years). PMT shows the date it ends and reminds managers before it does.
2. **Keep in stock**, or **Assign to someone** [[2]]. When assigning, pick the person, their project (only their projects are listed) and the condition it's given in.
3. Click **Save asset**.
