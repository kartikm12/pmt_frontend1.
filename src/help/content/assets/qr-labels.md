![QR label](1-label.webp "The label with the tag and name")

1. Open the asset and click **QR label**.
2. **Print label** [[1]], or **Download PNG** [[2]] to print it later.
3. Stick it on the asset. Scanning it with a phone opens the asset's page (after signing in).
