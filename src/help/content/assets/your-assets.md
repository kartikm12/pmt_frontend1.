**Assets** shows the company things you have.

## 1. Confirm you received it

![Your assets](1-yours.webp "A new laptop waiting for you to confirm")

When something is assigned to you it says **Not confirmed** [[1]]. Once you have it in your hands, click **I received it** [[2]]. Your manager is told.

## 2. Report a problem

![Report a problem](2-problem.webp "Say what's wrong, and add a photo if it helps")

1. Click **Report a problem** (or **Something's wrong** if you haven't confirmed it yet).
2. Write what's wrong and since when [[1]]. Add a photo if it helps [[2]].
3. Click **Send report** [[3]]. Your manager gets a notification and an email.

:::note Giving it back
To return something, talk to your manager. They record the return in PMT.
:::
