Managers see **Register** and **Your assets** at the top of **Assets**.

## 1. The register

![The asset register](1-register.webp "Totals, filters, and one row per asset")

- [[1]] **Total**, **Assigned**, **In stock**, **In repair** and **Lost**.
- [[2]] Search by tag, name, brand or serial, and filter by category, status or warranty.
- [[3]] Each asset's tag, who has it, its status, and when the warranty ends (orange when it ends within 30 days).
- [[4]] **Add asset**. **Recently deleted** brings back deleted assets.

Admins see every asset. Other managers see the assets of the people who [report to them](/help/team/reports-to) and the ones in stock.

## 2. One asset

![An asset's page](2-asset.webp "Who has it, its details, photos and history")

Click an asset to see who has it and whether they confirmed it [[1]], its details [[2]], what you can do with it [[3]], and its full **History** [[4]].
