## 1. The numbers at the top

![The numbers on the Dashboard](1-numbers.webp "Projects and tasks, counted")

- [[1]] **Projects** by status: all of them, active, pending (planning or on hold) and completed.
- [[2]] **Tasks**: all, still open, overdue and completed.
- [[3]] **Overdue tasks** shows *Needs attention* in red when anything is late.

Click the small picture in the corner of a card to open that list, for example the overdue tasks.

Managers see everything in the company. Everyone else sees the projects and tasks they're part of.

## 2. Projects Hub and Upcoming Deadlines

![Projects Hub and Upcoming Deadlines](2-lower.webp "Your projects, and what's due next")

- [[1]] **Projects Hub**: each project, its people and its status. Click one to open it.
- [[2]] **Upcoming Deadlines**: the next tasks due, soonest first, with their priority.

**View all** opens the full Projects or Tasks list.
