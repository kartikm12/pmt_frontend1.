## 1. Change the status

![Edit Project](1-status.webp "Status is in Edit Project")

1. Open the project and click **Edit Project**.
2. Pick the **Status** [[1]]: Planning, Active, On Hold, Completed or Cancelled.
3. Click **Save Changes** [[2]].

:::warning Completed and Cancelled lock the project
Nobody can add, move or change tasks in a completed or cancelled project. To work in it again, set it back to **Active**.
:::

## 2. Delete a project

Click **Delete** next to **Edit Project**, or **Delete Project** in the card's **⋮** menu on the Projects list. It goes to [Recently deleted](/help/admin/recently-deleted) with its tasks, for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone.
