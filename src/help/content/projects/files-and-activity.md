## 1. Files

![The Files tab](1-files.webp "Drag files in, or click Upload File")

Open the project and click **Files**. Click **Upload File** [[1]], drag files into the box, or paste a screenshot with **Ctrl V** [[2]]. Click a file to open it.

Files for one task belong on that task instead: see [Checklist, comments and files](/help/tasks/checklist-and-comments).

## 2. Activity

![The Activity tab](2-activity.webp "Newest first")

**Activity** lists everything that happened in the project: tasks added and changed, comments, rules and more, with who did it and when.
