## 1. Open the form

![The New Project button](1-button.webp "On the Projects list")

Go to **Projects** and click **New Project** [[1]].

## 2. Fill it in

![Create New Project](2-form.webp "Name, team and dates")

1. **Project Name** and a short **Description** [[1]].
2. **Project Team**: pick everyone who works on it [[2]]. At least one person is needed. You can add more later.
3. **Start Date** and **End Date** [[3]].
4. Click **Create Project** [[4]].

The project opens with four board columns: To Do, In Progress, Review and Completed. You can rename them or add your own: see [Board columns](/help/projects/board-columns).
