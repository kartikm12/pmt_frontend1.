Open a project and click the **Timeline** tab. There are two views.

## 1. Due dates

![Timeline by due dates](1-due-dates.webp "Each bar runs from a task's start to its due date")

- [[1]] Switch between **Due dates** and **Stages**.
- [[2]] Each bar runs from the task's start to its deadline. The colour is its stage now.
The red line is today. A bar that ends left of it and isn't green is late.

## 2. Stages

![Timeline by stages](2-stages.webp "Where each task spent its time")

Each task becomes a line of coloured pieces, one for each stage it was in, in order. Going back (for example from Review to In Progress) shows as another piece.

Click a task to see the exact dates, for example *Nov 10–12 To Do, 2 days*, and the total days in each stage. It shows where work waits longest.
