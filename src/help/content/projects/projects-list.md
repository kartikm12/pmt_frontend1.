![The Projects list](1-list.webp "Every project you're part of, as cards")

1. Search by name [[1]] and click **Search**.
2. Show only one status, such as *Active* or *Completed* [[2]].
3. Each card shows the due date, the people and the status [[3]]. Click the name to open the project.

Managers see every project. Everyone else sees the projects they're a member of, or have a task in.

:::note Project statuses
**Planning** (not started yet), **Active**, **On Hold**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**. A completed or cancelled project can be read but not changed until it's reopened.
:::
