Open a project to see its board. Each column is a stage of the work, and each card is a task.

## 1. Read the board

![A project board](1-board.webp "Columns from left to right, a card for each task")

- [[1]] The tabs: **Board**, **Timeline**, **Team**, **Files**, **Activity** and **Rules**.
- [[2]] A column, with how many tasks are in it.
- [[3]] A card: its title, priority, labels, the people on it, checklist progress and due date. A title in red means it's overdue.
- [[4]] **New Task** adds a task to this project (if you manage the project).

## 2. Move a card

Drag a card to another column when the work moves on, for example from *In Progress* to *Review*. Everyone sees the change straight away, and the task's history records it.

With the keyboard: click a card, press **Space** to pick it up, use the arrow keys to move it, and press **Space** again to drop it.

:::note Who can move cards
Anyone on the project can move cards, except people with **View only** access. Nothing can be moved while the project is Completed or Cancelled.
:::

Click a card's title to open the full [task page](/help/tasks/task-page).
