![The Calendar](1-calendar.webp "Each task sits on its due day")

- [[1]] Go to the month before or after, or back to **Today**.
- [[2]] **My tasks**, a project or a person: show only those deadlines.
- [[3]] Each task on its due day. The dot is its stage colour; finished tasks are crossed out. Today is circled.

Click a task to open it.

## Move a deadline

Drag a task to another day to change its due date. Everyone on the task is told. You need to be able to change the task (project managers, and people working on it).
