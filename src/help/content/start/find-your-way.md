## 1. Search for anything

![The search box with results](1-search.webp "Tasks, projects and people that match what you type")

1. Click **Search** at the top, or press **Ctrl K** (Cmd K on a Mac).
2. Type part of a name [[1]].
3. Results are grouped into tasks, projects and people [[2]]. Click one, or use the arrow keys and **Enter**.
4. Press **Esc** to close.

## 2. Get back

The line of links above every page title (for example *Projects › Customer Portal*) shows where you are. Click any part of it to go back up.

:::tip Keyboard shortcuts
**Settings → Shortcuts** lists them all. For example, on the board press **Space** to pick up a card and the arrow keys to move it.
:::
