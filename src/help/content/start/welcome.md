PMT is where your company plans projects, shares out the work and keeps track of company laptops and phones. Everyone sees the same live picture: when someone moves a task, you see it straight away.

## The parts of the screen

![The main screen with the menu on the left](1-screen.webp "The menu on the left, search, notifications and your name at the top")

- [[1]] **The menu.** Every screen you can use is listed here. You only see what your role allows.
- [[2]] **Search.** Find any task, project or person. Press **Ctrl K** from anywhere to jump here.
- [[3]] **Light or dark.** Switch the colours of the app.
- [[4]] **Notifications.** The red number is how many you haven't read yet.
- [[5]] **You.** Your name and role.

## Where to start

- New here? Read [Signing in](/help/start/sign-in) and [Finding your way](/help/start/find-your-way).
- Working on tasks? Start with [The board](/help/projects/board) and [The task page](/help/tasks/task-page).
- Got a company laptop or phone? See [Your assets](/help/assets/your-assets).

:::note What you see depends on your role
Admins, Managers and Project Managers see more screens (for example adding people, the asset register and Recently deleted). This Help only shows the guides for what you can do.
:::
