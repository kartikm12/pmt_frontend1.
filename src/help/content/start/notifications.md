PMT tells you when something needs you: a task is assigned to you, someone mentions you with **@**, a deadline is close or passed, or (for managers) someone signs up or reports a problem with a laptop.

## 1. The bell

![The bell opened](1-bell.webp "The newest notifications, straight from the bell")

1. Click the bell [[1]]. The red number is how many are unread.
2. Click a notification to open the task, project or asset it's about.
3. **View All Notifications** [[2]] opens the full list.

## 2. The full list

![The notifications page](2-list.webp "All or only unread, and mark everything read at once")

- **All** and **Unread** [[1]] switch what you see.
- **Mark all as read** [[2]] clears the red number.
- Point at one notification to mark it read or delete it.

:::note Emails too
You also get emails about your work. To stop them, turn off **Email me about my work** in **Settings → Notifications**. Account emails, such as password resets, always come.
:::
