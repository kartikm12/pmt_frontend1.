## 1. Sign in

![The sign-in screen](1-sign-in.webp "Sign in with your work email and password")

1. Type your work email and password [[1]].
2. Click **Sign in to Dashboard** [[3]].

Forgot it? Click **Forgot password?** [[2]].

## 2. Reset a forgotten password

![Forgot your password](2-forgot.webp "We email you a link to choose a new password")

1. Type your email and click **Send reset link** [[1]].
2. Open the email and choose a new password (at least 8 characters).
3. Sign in with the new password.

:::tip Change your password any time
Signed in already? Go to **Settings → Password**. See [Settings](/help/settings/settings).
:::

## Signing up yourself

If your manager didn't add you, click **Need an account? Register** on the sign-in screen. You can't sign in straight away: an Admin, Manager or Project Manager has to approve you first. You'll get an email when they do.

:::faq Why was I signed out?
You can be signed in on one device at a time. Signing in somewhere else signs you out here. You're also signed out if a manager changes your role or suspends you.
:::

:::faq It says "The server is busy right now"
PMT couldn't reach its database for a moment. Wait a few seconds and try again. Your password is fine.
:::
