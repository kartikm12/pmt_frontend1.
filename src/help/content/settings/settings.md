## 1. Profile

![Settings, Profile](1-profile.webp "Your photo and details")

- **Change photo** [[1]]: a PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP up to 2 MB.
- Your name, job title, phone and a short bio [[2]]. Click **Save Changes**.

Your role is set by a manager.

## 2. Password

![Settings, Password](2-password.webp "Your current password, then the new one twice")

Type your current password and the new one [[1]], then save. You get an email to say it changed.

## 3. Notifications and Appearance

![Settings, Notifications](3-notifications.webp "Turn work emails on or off")

- **Notifications**: **Email me about my work** [[1]] covers new tasks, deadlines and @mentions. In-app notifications always stay on.
- **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Match device**.
- **Shortcuts** lists the keyboard shortcuts.
