![Recently deleted](1-trash.webp "Deleted projects and tasks, for 30 days")

1. Go to **Recently deleted** in the menu.
2. Find the project or task [[1]]. Each shows how many days are left before it's removed.
3. Click **Restore** [[2]]. A project comes back with its tasks and team.

After 30 days items are removed for good. Deleted assets are brought back from **Assets → Recently deleted** instead.
