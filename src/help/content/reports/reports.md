## 1. Choose what to look at

![The top of Reports](1-top.webp "The period, a project and a person")

1. Pick the period: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** [[1]].
2. Narrow it to one project or one person [[2]].
3. **CSV** downloads the numbers; **Print / PDF** prints the page or saves it as a PDF [[3]].

The four numbers [[4]] compare with the period before: tasks **Completed**, **New tasks**, how many **Finished on time**, and what's **Overdue now**.

The chart below shows new and completed tasks per day. If more come in than are finished, the backlog is growing.

## 2. Project health and who's busy

![Project health and open work](2-health.webp "Problems first")

- [[1]] **Project health**: each active project's progress, overdue tasks and status. *At risk* means it has overdue tasks or is behind schedule.
- [[2]] **Where the work stands**: how many tasks are in each stage.
- [[3]] **Open work per person**: the red part is overdue. Click a person to see their tasks.

At the bottom, **Needs attention** lists overdue tasks, oldest first.
