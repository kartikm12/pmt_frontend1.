## How the score works

![How the score works](2-score.webp "Points for tasks, and for projects you manage")

- A finished task gives points, with extra for finishing before the deadline and for Urgent or High tasks.
- Each overdue task takes points away.
- Project managers also score for the projects they finish.
- Only work finished inside the chosen period counts, and a score never goes below 0.
